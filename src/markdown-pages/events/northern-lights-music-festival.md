**Bringing a Piece of the Pacific Northwest Down to SoCal!**

Welcome to the Northern Lights Music Festival! Keeping true to our roots from Seattle, WA with Northwest IdolFest, we are happy to be providing a jam-packed showcase with some of the best up-and-coming kaigai idols from across the country (and across the world!). 

[Register](https://idolfes.us/register) for a badge today to make sure you don't miss this lineup!

- - -

**Introducing our 2026 Northern Lights Lineup!**

![](/images/uploads/airi_star.png "AIRI")

Born in New York and sailing from Scotland, AIRI makes her international debut at USIF! 🌊✨
This oceanic idol blends j/kpop influences, an aggressive rap flow, and her sweet siren voice to bring you an enchanting experience! Joining her from Scotland is celestial dragon bassist, Ryu! Ryu commands the stage with their bass guitar and fiery persona. 
Together, this dynamic duo is an international pair you don’t want to miss! Come along for some fun covers, as well as original music!

![](/images/uploads/cyberedge_star.png "CYberEdGe")

Brought together by a common love for anisong, ani idol, and video game music, CYberEdGe is a SoCal-based instrumental and vocalist duo composed of Cy and eggu. With Cy's guitar and eggu's powerful voice, combined they create high energy, hard rock arrangements of iconic songs from their favorite genres.

Together, we kindle our dreams ablaze, against all odds.

HiJinx! is a group of monster idols that are here to bring the Halloween spirit all year round! Their goal is to have fun and inspire others to become idols themselves! With self made choreography and j-rock inspired sound, they are ready to make you scream!!!
