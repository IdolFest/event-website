**Introducing our 2026 Virtual Stardust Lineup!**

### Beni Doki

![](/images/uploads/beni-doki_star_2026.png "Beni Doki")

The tomboy Fire Captain of the PROMISU Metro City! Beni is a hardworking fire dragon who definitely sometimes starts the fires herself (on ACCIDENT), but always puts them out as efficiently as possible! When she's not taking care of the city and her crew, she streams on the side to help make up for all the fire damage she causes.

- - -

### Dreamy Etude

![](/images/uploads/dreamy-etude_star_2026.png "Dreamy Etude")

Dreamy Etude is your local neighborhood magical girl, who is working her way to paying off all her gacha debt with too many jobs to count. Catch her at your local maid cafe or as an idol trainee working hard to bring laughs and smiles. 

- - -

### PRISMIA

![](/images/uploads/prismia_star_2026.png "Prismia")

PRISMIA is the flagship 1st Generation of Takara Live, bringing together a brilliant spectrum of virtual talent: Sansha Safaia, Ruvii Redcore, Elaine Duvelle, Hisui Hotaru, and Lumiel Lumiere. Representing unique gems and archetypes, PRISMIA delivers high-energy musical performances, engaging content, and unforgettable interactive experiences for fans around the globe.

- - -

### Stella Fos

![](/images/uploads/beni-doki_star_2026.png "Stella Fos")

Heya Helium Huffers! Debuted in 2021, Stella Fos is wishing star vsinger and variety streamer with inexhaustive energy to yap and sing for hours on end. They long to be a light in the dark for others with their humor and voice, singing to connect with their community around the world.

- - -

### Tifamin

![](/images/uploads/tifamin_star_2026.png "Tifamin")

Tifamin might be soft spoken, but she's hiding a BIG singing voice!

This expressive vocalist brings over 12 years of live performance experience into the online scene.

- - -

### tsubasa83

![](/images/uploads/tsubasa83_star_2026.png "tsubasa83")

From her world to ours, tsubasa83 is a post-apocalyptic vtuber whose interest in idol and wota culture turned her from survivor to dreamer; and now, idol! Taking inspiration from anisong regulars like EGOIST and LiSA, as well as her first oshi (ALSO named Tsubasa), she performs music that can get you moving -- or crying! Either way's fine; all she wants to do is spread color and hope to all the survivors of the world -- including you!

- - -

### Vampirate Odessa

![](/images/uploads/odessa_star_2026.png "Vampirate Odessa")

Say ahoy to your vampire pirate captain, Vampirate Odessa! Captivating her crew with the voice of a siren, Odessa's singing brings people together from all around the world. As a variety streamer, she entertains her crew on YouTube and Twitch with lively chats, delightfully scuffed gaming, and karaoke streams with plenty of sea shanties. She is delighted to return to the Virtual Stardust stage for a second time to share her love of idols and pump up the US IdolFes crowd!! Once you hear her voice, you'll be a crewmate for life!
